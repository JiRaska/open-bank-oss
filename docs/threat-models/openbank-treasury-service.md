# Threat model — openbank-treasury-service

- **Status:** MVP (ADR-0315 D1–D6, D9), sandbox only — money-market deals against a SYNTHETIC counterparty set
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-10
- **Owner:** treasury-service CODEOWNERS
- **Related ADRs:** ADR-0003, ADR-0030, ADR-0031, ADR-0034, ADR-0313, ADR-0314, ADR-0315

## Scope and assets

The bank's own money-market book: MM placements with other banks (`MM_PLACEMENT`), MM borrowings
from other banks (`MM_BORROWING`), the ČNB overnight deposit facility (`CNB_DEPOSIT_FACILITY`) and
overnight borrowing from the ČNB marginal lending (lombard) facility (`CNB_LOMBARD`).
A deal moves `DRAFT → PENDING_APPROVAL → BOOKED → CONFIRMED → SETTLED → MATURED`, or to
`CANCELLED` (before booking) or `REVERSED` (after). Every settlement, maturity and reversal of a settled deal posts a
balanced journal to the ledger through its public API. **Money-path** (`rules.yaml:
money_path_services`): a wrong or duplicated posting misstates the bank's own balance sheet and
its liquidity (1510 is the risk engine's only HQLA account).

Assets: the deal book and its append-only timeline (`deal_transitions`), the counterparty master
and its credit limits, the ledger journals the deals produce (`deal_journals` holds their ids and
idempotency keys), and the `openbank.treasury.deal.events` stream the audit trail records.
Counterparties are banks and the central bank — no natural persons, no customer data.

## Trust boundaries

1. Treasury staff call `/api/v1/treasury/*` through admin-ui's BFF with their OWN bearer token.
   `ROLE_TREASURY_DEALER` drafts, submits and cancels; `ROLE_TREASURY_APPROVER` approves, rejects,
   confirms (records the counterparty's confirmation, ADR-0315 D2), settles, matures and reverses. RBAC (`TreasuryResource`), then OPA (`treasury_rest_ext.rego`,
   human principals only, every service-account excluded), then the domain (`Deal`).
   `GET /limits/utilisation` (#10896) is read-only and gated by the SAME `treasury.counterparty.read`
   action/roles as `GET /counterparties` — no new rego rule. Its `utilised`/`breached` fields are
   never computed independently: both it and the booking-time limit check read
   `DealRepository.exposure`, whose state/product filter derives from the single
   `Deal.LIMIT_CONSUMING_STATES` constant, so the view cannot silently diverge from what actually
   blocks booking.
   Deal reads (`GET /deals/{id}`) gained an additive `history[].limitSnapshot` (API 1.16.0): the
   structured figures (limit, currency, exposure after, headroom after) of the limit check a
   submit/approve transition already recorded in its note. `LimitNote.parse` is the single reader of
   the one format `LimitNote.format` writes and returns null for any other note, so no new data is
   stored or exposed — the figures were already readable in the note text by the same roles under
   the same `treasury.deal.read` gate — and no new endpoint, input or write path exists.
2. The service posts journals to ledger-service's private-CA mTLS listener (8443, client
   certificate `treasury-internal-tls`) with its OWN machine identity: the named oidc-client `m2m`
   = Keycloak client `openbank-treasury`, ROLE_API only (`LedgerRestClient`,
   `@OidcClientFilter("m2m")`). `ledger_rest_ext.rego` grants `service-account-openbank-treasury`
   exactly `ledger.create` — no reverse, read, trigger or close action.
3. Deal events leave through the transactional outbox (`TreasuryOutboxDispatcher`) over mTLS
   Kafka as KafkaUser `treasury-service`, Write on its one topic only. audit-service reads it.
4. The in-process simulated market (`SimulatedMarketScheduler`, `Actor.SIMULATED_MARKET`) stands in
   for counterparty confirmation and settlement (ADR-0315 D9): it confirms BOOKED deals (the
   event says `simulated: true`) before it settles — with quotes on, a quoted money-market deal
   only when struck at the counterparty's quote.
5. With quotes on (ADR-0315 D9) the service reads the risk engine's curve sets over its private-CA
   mTLS listener (8443, client certificate `treasury-internal-tls`) as its OWN m2m identity
   (`openbank-treasury`); `risk_rest_ext.rego` grants that principal `risk.curve-set.read` only. It is SYNTHETIC; real market
   connectivity does not exist.

## Posting table (ADR-0315 D5)

Key `treasury:<dealId>:<event>`, balanced per currency, `transactionId` = deal id. CZK / EUR
accounts; the ČNB facility is CZK only. Seeded by ledger migration
`V29__treasury_money_market_accounts.sql` with fixed ids `a0000000-0000-0000-0000-00000000<code>`.

| Product | SETTLED (value date) | MATURED (maturity date) |
|---|---|---|
| `MM_PLACEMENT` | Dr 1500/1501 placements · Cr nostro 1001/1002 — P | Dr nostro P+I · Cr 1500/1501 P · Cr 4200/4201 MM interest income I |
| `CNB_DEPOSIT_FACILITY` | Dr 1510 deposit facility at ČNB · Cr 1001 — P | Dr 1001 P+I · Cr 1510 P · Cr 4200 I |
| `MM_BORROWING` | Dr nostro · Cr 2300/2301 borrowings — P | Dr 2300/2301 P · Dr 5200/5201 MM interest expense I · Cr nostro P+I |
| `CNB_LOMBARD` | Dr 1001 · Cr 2320 borrowings from ČNB — P | Dr 2320 P · Dr 5200 I · Cr 1001 P+I |

`BOOKED` posts nothing (no off-balance commitment in the MVP). `REVERSED` from `SETTLED` posts the
settlement journal with every side flipped under key `...:reversed`; from `BOOKED` it posts
nothing. I = principal × rate/100 × days/360 (ACT/360), half-up to 2 dp; a zero-rate deal posts no
interest line. 1520/1521 and 2310/2311 (accrued interest) are seeded but unused until daily
accrual lands. Implemented in `PostingRules`, asserted row by row in `PostingRulesTest`.

## STRIDE analysis

| Threat | Control | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|
| Spoofing — a non-person books a deal | `Deal.approve` refuses any actor whose type is not HUMAN (`ActorNotPermittedException`, 403); `Actor.fromPrincipalName` classifies `agent:` as AI_AGENT and `service-account-` as SERVICE, the interceptor's own convention; `treasury_rest_ext.rego` excludes every service-account and grants AI_AGENT nothing (an agent's only grant is its agents.yaml charter); DB CHECK `deals_approver_human` | Principal classification is by name prefix; a human account named `agent:…` would be treated as an agent (fail-closed) |
| Elevation — self-approval (four-eyes) | Enforced in the domain, not only in OPA: `Deal.approve` throws `FourEyesViolationException` (422) when the approver is the creator or the submitter; DB CHECK `deals_four_eyes`; `TreasuryDealApiIT` proves a user holding BOTH roles still gets 422 | Identity is the token's principal name; two accounts held by one person defeat any four-eyes control |
| Elevation — AI agent books, confirms, settles or reverses | ADR-0315 D3: an agent may only draft, with a rationale; submit/approve/reject/cancel/confirm/settle/mature/reverse all require HUMAN (SYSTEM additionally for settle/mature by the simulated market); `DealTest` and `TreasuryDealApiIT` run as `agent:treasury-drafter` holding ROLE_TREASURY_APPROVER and assert 403 | The `treasury-dealing-assistant` charter (ADR-0315 D10) grants the three reads and `treasury.deal.draft` only and denies every other deal action and `treasury.nostro.*`; `build-bundle.sh` asserts it against the real charter data and `TreasuryAgentDraftIT` runs as `agent:treasury-dealing-assistant` (dealer role): draft 201, submit/approve/override/settle/reverse 403. The charter ships `enabled: false` (no runtime yet) — OPA does not read `enabled`, so the grant is live policy |
| Tampering — a deal booked beyond the counterparty limit | `LimitCheck` at submission AND again at approval over outstanding placed principal (PENDING_APPROVAL/BOOKED/SETTLED placements and ČNB deposits); a breach throws `LimitBreachedException` (422) and nothing books | Product limits (ADR-0315 D4, 2026-10-03) now bound currency, principal and tenor per product, enforced at submit and re-checked at approval; ADR-0313 risk limits are still not checked; two concurrent approvals could each see the other's exposure as absent |
| Tampering — a double or partial posting | The journal is posted BEFORE the state change commits, under a stable key; ledger's `postJournal` returns the original entry for a repeated key; deal row, timeline, journal id and outbox event commit in ONE transaction (`DealRepositoryImpl.save`); `deal_journals.idempotency_key` is UNIQUE. `TreasuryDealApiIT` loses the ledger's response after commit and proves the retry yields ONE journal | A lost response leaves the journal in the ledger while the deal still reads BOOKED until someone (or the simulated market) retries |
| Tampering — posting before the deal is booked | Only `settle`, `mature` and `reverse` call `LedgerPostingPort`; each is reachable only from BOOKED or SETTLED (`Deal.settle` / `Deal.mature` / `Deal.reverse` state checks); the IT asserts zero ledger calls through draft, submit, a refused self-approval, an agent's attempt and the approval itself | — |
| Tampering — wrong GL account | Accounts are fixed ids from one table (`TreasuryChart`); the ledger rejects (422) a line whose currency does not match its account; CZK nostro 1001 is re-keyed to its fixed id only while unreferenced, else the CZK posting fails loudly rather than landing elsewhere | An environment where 1001 was already referenced needs a reviewed manual re-key before CZK deals can settle |
| Repudiation — who did what | `deal_transitions` is append-only (one row per transition: actor id, actor type, time, note incl. the limit figures and any rejection/reversal reason); `treasury.deal.*` events carry `createdBy`/`approvedBy`/`reversedBy` and `sourceService` and reach the tamper-evident audit trail (`audit-money-path-subscription`) | The ledger records the service's fixed actor id (`LedgerPostingAdapter.SYSTEM_ACTOR`), not the human approver; the link is the deal id (`transactionId`) |
| Information disclosure | ClusterIP only, NetworkPolicy allow-list, mTLS to ledger and Kafka; reads require a treasury role or ROLE_ADMIN; no customer data is held. OIDC token-issuer TLS verification stays at the default `required` outside `%dev` (security review HIGH, fixed 2026-09-25) | Counterparty limits and the whole book are visible to every dealer |
| Denial of service | The simulated market runs one pass at a time (`ConcurrentExecution.SKIP`), counts a failing deal and continues; outbox dispatch is claimed with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`; 1 MB body limit | The ledger call is synchronous inside the request; a slow ledger holds the approver's request |

## Invariants

1. No journal is posted for a deal that has not been BOOKED by a second human.
2. A deal's approver is never its creator or submitter, and is always HUMAN (domain + DB CHECK).
3. Every journal key is `treasury:<dealId>:<settled|matured|reversed>`; each is posted at most
   once per deal in the ledger, however often it is retried.
4. Every posted journal balances per currency.

## Out of scope / follow-ups

Senior-approver limit override (ADR-0315 D4), product and ADR-0313 risk limits, daily accrual
postings to 1520/1521/2310/2311, a holiday calendar, the agent charter (D10),
minimum reserves (D8), and real market connectivity (D9).
- **2026-10-01** — **Daily position: actual vs projected, Prague bank day (#11708).** `GET /api/v1/treasury/positions` keeps its route, role and OPA action (`treasury.position.read`); the response gains `today`, `basis` (`ACTUAL`|`PROJECTED`), `countedStates` and per-currency `dealCount` (openapi 1.13.0 → 1.14.0, additive). The default date and the actual/projected split come from `AccountingClock.bank(clock)` (Europe/Prague), no longer the pod's UTC default zone. `asOf <= today` counts SETTLED/MATURED only; a future `asOf` also counts BOOKED/CONFIRMED deals on their contracted dates. **STRIDE-I:** the projection exposes concluded-but-unsettled deals to the same readers who could already list them via `treasury.deal.read`; nothing new is disclosed. **STRIDE-T/R:** read-only, posts nothing, changes no limit; `basis` is stated in the response so a projected figure cannot be read as booked fact. **Residual:** a projection assumes no new deals, cancellations or reversals. Rollback: revert the PR; no migration.
- **2026-09-26** — **Senior override of a counterparty-limit breach (ADR-0315 D4).** `POST /api/v1/treasury/deals/{id}/override-limit` (required `Idempotency-Key`, `reason` body) records an override on a PENDING_APPROVAL deal whose limit check is breached. It needs a new realm role, `ROLE_TREASURY_SENIOR_APPROVER`, and its own OPA action, `treasury.deal.override-limit`, granted only to that role for a HUMAN principal that is not a `service-account-`. A senior gets read, and neither drafts nor books. The domain enforces the rest, not OPA alone. The override is human-only, carries a non-blank reason and exists only for a breached check. The overrider is never the deal's creator or submitter, and the senior who overrode may not also book the deal, so a breach booking involves three people. The override is bounded to the exposure it was granted for (`coversExposureUpTo`): if exposure grows before booking, approval refuses again. A rejection drops it. **STRIDE-E:** closed by the separate role and action, the service-account and agent exclusions (`opa test`: dealer, approver, admin, a machine and an agent holding the senior role are all denied), and the domain four-eyes rules. **STRIDE-R:** who, why, when, the exposure covered and the limit are persisted (V4, complete-or-absent CHECK) and written to the deal timeline. Rollback: remove the endpoint and the rego rule; V4 columns are nullable.
- **2026-09-27** — **AI dealing assistant drafts, never books (ADR-0315 D10, #10896).** A `treasury-dealing-assistant` charter in `agents.yaml` allows `treasury.deal.read`, `treasury.counterparty.read`, `treasury.position.read` and `treasury.deal.draft`, and explicitly denies submit, approve, reject, cancel, settle, mature, reverse, override-limit and `treasury.nostro.*`. The grant reaches treasury only through base `rest.rego`'s `agent-charter-allows` → `agents.allow`; `treasury_rest_ext.rego` still grants an agent nothing, and its test fails if any treasury rule ever does. An agent's draft must now carry `inputs` (a JSON object of the data it read, V9 `draft_inputs`, nullable) as well as `rationale`, both returned on the deal so the submitting dealer and the booking approver see them. **STRIDE-E:** a draft consumes no limit and posts nothing; only a HUMAN may submit, and a second HUMAN books (four-eyes unchanged — an agent-created deal has an agent creator, so the submitter and approver are two distinct people). **STRIDE-R:** rationale and inputs persisted with the deal and the creator recorded as AI_AGENT on the timeline. **STRIDE-T residual:** `inputs` is what the agent *claims* it read; nothing re-derives it from the quotes and limits at draft time — the approver's limit check at submit/approve is recomputed from live data regardless. Rollback: drop the charter (the grant disappears with it); V9 column is nullable.
- **2026-09-27** — **ČNB lombard (marginal lending) borrowing, `CNB_LOMBARD` (#10896).** A new product on the existing deal lifecycle, no new endpoint, role or OPA action: it is drafted, four-eyes booked, settled, matured and reversed by exactly the `MM_BORROWING` code paths. The domain pins its terms (`Deal` init): CZK only, counterparty `CNB` (and no interbank product may face `CNB`), maturity forced to the next business day whatever the request says, and a rate strictly > 0 (the dealer enters the ČNB lombard rate; nothing fetches it). It posts to a new CZK-only liability, 2320 "Borrowings from CNB (lombard)", seeded by ledger migration `V30` with the V29 fixed-id convention; accrual and maturity use the MM expense and accrued-payable accounts. **Limits:** as a borrowing it consumes no credit limit (`LimitCheck` deal amount 0); the `CNB` counterparty row and its limit are the deposit facility's, unchanged. **STRIDE-T (wrong GL account):** the ledger refuses a 2320 line in EUR (422, `TreasuryAccountsPostingIT`); `PostingRulesTest` asserts the settlement, maturity, accrual and reversal rows. **Residual / not modelled:** the collateral pledge that backs a real lombard loan (eligible securities, haircuts, the pledge itself) is NOT modelled — a lombard deal here is unsecured on the books; the lombard rate is not validated against the ČNB's published rate; there is still no holiday calendar, so a Friday deal matures Monday and a pre-holiday deal matures on the holiday. Rollback: treasury `V7` and ledger `V30` carry their rollback notes (only while no `CNB_LOMBARD` deal / 2320 line exists).
- **2026-09-26** — **Nostro reconciliation (ADR-0315 D7, #10896).** `POST /api/v1/treasury/nostro/statements` (camt.053 XML, required `Idempotency-Key`, OPA `treasury.nostro.upload` — `ROLE_TREASURY_APPROVER`, human, never a `service-account-`) stores a correspondent's end-of-day statement for an account configured in `openbank.treasury.nostro.accounts` (IBAN -> nostro GL 1001/1002; a mapping to any other GL refuses to start). `GET /api/v1/treasury/nostro/statements/{id}/reconciliation` (`treasury.nostro.read`) compares it with the ledger's nostro GL over the statement's booking-date span (≤ 31 days) and lists matched items, unmatched items on BOTH sides and the opening/closing differences. **Nothing is posted**: the service holds no reconciling-entry path, and the new ledger grant (`service-treasury-ledger-read`, `ledger.list` + `ledger.read` for `service-account-openbank-treasury` only) is read-only. **STRIDE-T (untrusted XML):** DOCTYPE refused and external entities off (XXE; `Camt053ParserTest`), exactly one `Stmt`, balances must foot or the upload is a 400, 1 MB body limit. **STRIDE-T (false match):** exact match needs amount, direction, date AND reference, the reference equal to the ledger transaction id or to a WHOLE token run of the journal description (never a substring); the amount/date fallback pairs only a unique candidate on each side and is labelled `AMOUNT_DATE`; a tampered amount is unmatched on both sides (`NostroMatcherTest`, `NostroReconciliationApiIT`). **STRIDE-R:** the uploader, time and SHA-256 of the exact bytes are stored with the statement (V8); an Idempotency-Key replay with different bytes, or the same (IBAN, statement id) under a new key, is 409, so a statement cannot be silently replaced — including under a concurrent upload, where the unique constraints (not the pre-check) decide and the loser gets the same replay/409; amounts beyond NUMERIC(19,4) are a 400, never rounded. **STRIDE-S:** the statement is not signature-verified — anyone holding the approver role can upload a fabricated file; the uploader is recorded, the file is not authenticated (follow-up: correspondent channel / signature). **Initial residual (superseded by the native-currency phase below):** the ledger exposes only base-currency (CZK) balances, so a EUR nostro shows no ledger balance, no difference and `reconciled: null` with a `balanceNotStated` reason — items are still matched on native amounts, balances are not compared. reconciliation is computed on read against the live ledger, so a later back-dated journal changes the answer; synthetic (canary) journals are excluded on both the line and balance reads (ledger `REAL_ONLY`). Rollback: remove the endpoints and the two rego rules; V8 is additive (drop the two tables).
- **2026-09-26** — **FX spot deals (`FX_SPOT`, #10896).** The bank buys or sells EUR against CZK with a counterparty through the SAME lifecycle and endpoints as the money-market products: no new route, role or OPA action (draft/submit/approve/settle/reverse reuse `treasury.deal.*`). `currency`/`principal` are the foreign leg, `rate` the dealer's rate in CZK per unit; the CZK leg is `principal × rate`, half-up to 2 dp, stored (V9 `fx_counter_amount`) and re-validated on every load. Value date defaults to T+2 business days (weekends only — still no holiday calendar) and may not be later (a later date is a forward, not built). **Posting:** SETTLED posts ONE journal in two currencies through the ledger's existing FX position accounts (V5 of ledger-service, fixed ids 1990/1991): BUY = Dr 1002 / Cr 1991 (EUR) and Dr 1990 / Cr 1001 (CZK), SELL = the same lines flipped; balanced within each currency, key `treasury:<dealId>:settled`. No ledger migration was needed. A spot never accrues or matures (`maturity_date = value_date`, excluded from the simulated market's maturity pass, `mature` is 409); reversal from SETTLED posts the flipped four lines. **STRIDE-T (limit):** a spot carries settlement risk, so it consumes the counterparty's **CZK** limit by its CZK equivalent while PENDING_APPROVAL or BOOKED — the same `LimitCheck.of` and the same breach/override path, sharing one CZK line with CZK placements; it releases on settlement. **STRIDE-T (rate):** the dealer-entered rate can be checked against fx-service's mid within `openbank.treasury.fx-spot.rate-check.tolerance-percent`; outside tolerance the deal is FLAGGED on the deal and its timeline, never blocked, so the four-eyes approver sees it. **Residual:** treasury has no fx-service client yet, so the check is OFF by default and nothing validates the dealer's rate except the approver; switched on without a client, every FX deal is flagged "mid unavailable". **Out of scope:** revaluation of the open position (the ledger's own daily FX revaluation, ADR-0031, marks 1991 against ČNB fixings; treasury adds nothing), FX forwards and swaps, currencies other than EUR. Rollback: V9 columns are nullable; roll back only while no FX_SPOT row exists.
- **2026-09-27** — **Nostro balances in the statement currency (#11107, supersedes the base-currency residual above).** Reconciliation now reads both ledger balances from ledger's `GET /api/v1/journals/accounts/{code}/balance?asOf=&currency=` (native `amount`, never `base_amount`; ledger `ledger.read`, already granted to `service-account-openbank-treasury`) — one path for CZK and EUR: opening as at the day before the earliest booking date, closing as at the statement date. The trial-balance read is gone. **STRIDE-T (wrong figure compared):** the adapter refuses (500, no report) a response whose echoed code/currency/asOf differs from what it asked, so a balance for another account, currency or day can never produce a difference; the pact pins the literal path and the echo, replayed by ledger's `@PactFolder` provider test. **Residual:** `balanceNotStated` remains only for a ledger 404 on the GL (chart not migrated) — `reconciled: null`, never a pass; any other ledger failure fails the request. Rollback: revert the read path; V10 adds `opening_date` and must be removed only after preserved statement periods are accounted for. The ledger grant is unchanged.
- **2026-09-29** — **Counterparty confirmation, the `CONFIRMED` state (ADR-0315 D2).** `POST /api/v1/treasury/deals/{id}/confirm` (required `Idempotency-Key`, optional `{"reference"}` of at most 200 characters) moves a BOOKED deal to CONFIRMED and emits `treasury.deal.confirmed.v1` through the outbox in the same transaction. **Nothing posts.** New OPA action `treasury.deal.confirm`, granted in `treasury_rest_ext.rego` to `ROLE_TREASURY_APPROVER` only (the back office — never the dealer, the senior, an admin, a `service-account-`, or an agent); the `treasury-dealing-assistant` charter lists it under `tools.deny`, and `build-bundle.sh` asserts that against the real charter. **STRIDE-E:** the domain additionally refuses the deal's creator and submitter (422 `FOUR_EYES_VIOLATION`: the dealer who struck the trade does not also attest that the other side agreed) and any AI_AGENT or SERVICE principal (403), which holds with `AUTHZ_ENFORCE` off; `opa test` has a must-ALLOW (approver) and must-DENY (dealer, senior, admin, nobody, shared service-account, agent, chartered agent) for the action, and `TreasuryDealConfirmationIT` proves the 403/422s over HTTP. **STRIDE-T (settling an unconfirmed deal):** `Deal.settle` accepts CONFIRMED only; BOOKED answers 409 unless `openbank.treasury.confirmation.required=false`. **Backward compatibility — decided, not migrated:** existing BOOKED rows are NOT rewritten to CONFIRMED (that would record confirmations nobody received). With the default (`true`) such a deal waits for a confirm; in the sandbox the simulated market confirms it on its next pass, as SYSTEM, labelled `simulated: true` — a SYNTHETIC confirmation. `false` is the escape hatch for a deployment without a confirmation step and restores settle-from-BOOKED exactly (`TreasuryConfirmationNotRequiredIT`). **Limits:** CONFIRMED is on-book (`LIMIT_CONSUMING_STATES`, `FX_LIMIT_CONSUMING_STATES`), so confirming never releases limit. **STRIDE-R:** the confirmer, time and reference are on the timeline; the event carries `confirmedBy` and `simulated`. **Residual:** a confirmation is attested by a person, not matched against a counterparty message (MT300/MT320, or a platform) — real confirmation matching stays external (ADR-0313); the risk engine ignores the new type (counted `ignored`, not `malformed`). Rollback: treasury `V13` carries its rollback note (only while no CONFIRMED row exists); remove the endpoint and the rego action.
- **2026-09-29** — **Simulated counterparties quote off the risk engine's curve set (ADR-0315 D9).** `GET /api/v1/treasury/quotes?product=&currency=&tenorDays=` (OPA `treasury.quote.read`, the same treasury staff as every read; the `treasury-dealing-assistant` charter allows it — a read of synthetic prices, the picture the charter was written to assemble) returns each SYNTHETIC bank counterparty's bid/ask: the latest curve set's CZEONIA (CZK) or ESTR (EUR) zero curve, linear on zero rates and flat outside the pillars, turned into ACT/360 simple percent, ± a configured half-spread (`openbank.treasury.simulated-market.quotes.spread-bp`). `synthetic: true` on the board and on every quote; a non-synthetic counterparty (ČNB) or one without a spread never quotes. Only while the simulated market AND `quotes.enabled` are on (409 otherwise). **Confirmation:** with quotes on, the simulated counterparty confirms a quoted MM deal only when it was struck at its quote or better for it (placement ≤ bid, borrowing ≥ ask) and otherwise DECLINES it — the deal stays BOOKED for a person to confirm or reverse; unquoted deals (ČNB facilities, FX spot) confirm as before. **New edge (STRIDE-S/E):** treasury → risk-engine 8443 as `service-account-openbank-treasury`, granted `risk.curve-set.read` only (see the risk-engine threat model); consumer pact with literal paths and a 401 interaction, replayed by the risk engine on every PR. **STRIDE-T (a wrong price confirms a deal):** the price is only as good as the curve set, which a person uploaded (`risk.curve-set.create`, real staff only); a declined deal is never settled by the market, and nothing posts on confirmation either way. **STRIDE-D / fail-closed:** an unreachable, refusing or empty risk engine is 503 `QUOTE_UNAVAILABLE` on the endpoint and a COUNTED failure in the market pass (the deal stays BOOKED, liveness is not recorded) — never "no curve, so confirm". **Residual:** a quote is priced at confirmation time against the NEWEST curve set, not the one live when the deal was struck, and the tenor runs from the curve's as-of date (no forward start); the quotes switch is `false` in the sandbox manifest until the grant, network edge and TLS bucket are verified live. Rollback: switch `OPENBANK_TREASURY_SIMULATED_QUOTES_ENABLED` off (the market reverts to confirming every BOOKED deal), then remove the endpoint and the rego rules.
- **2026-10-01** — **Nostro breaks with age, the break-aged alert, and MT940 upload (ADR-0315 D7).** Every item a reconciliation leaves unmatched — a statement line or a ledger line on the nostro GL — is persisted as a break (V14 `nostro_breaks`, unique `break_key` per item) with the day it was first seen; its age is counted in business days (Mon–Fri, no holiday calendar — a holiday can only make a break read OLDER, i.e. alert early). A later reconciliation that matches it sets `resolved_on`. An hourly `@Scheduled` sweep (suspend, `treasury-nostro-break-sweep` liveness) re-reconciles statements closing in the last 31 days, then marks each OPEN break at least `openbank.treasury.nostro.break-alert-age-days` (default 3) old AND at least `break-alert-min-amount` in its own currency as aged. **Nothing is posted**: breaks are read-only records for a person. `GET /api/v1/treasury/nostro/{account}/breaks` reuses `treasury.nostro.read`, and `POST /api/v1/treasury/nostro/statements/mt940` reuses `treasury.nostro.upload` — no new OPA action, role or grant, so the existing must-ALLOW/must-DENY policy tests cover both routes (a dealer reads breaks and is refused the MT940 upload; `NostroReconciliationApiIT`). **STRIDE-R / exactly-once alert:** an aged break is stamped `alerted_at` by a conditional UPDATE (`... where alerted_at is null`) and the `treasury.nostro.break-aged.v1` outbox row is written in the SAME transaction, so two sweeping pods (an Argo canary) announce a break once (`NostroBreakSweepIT`, driven by the real scheduler). **STRIDE-I:** the event carries amount, side, dates and the bank's own nostro IBAN, but no reference or `:86:` narrative — remittance text can name a customer and the topic is read by audit-service and the risk engine (which counts the type `ignored`). The break listing does return the reference, to treasury staff only, as the reconciliation already did. **STRIDE-T (untrusted MT940):** a pure domain parser; strict UTF-8, every control character except CR/LF refused (so a NUL never reaches Postgres), 1 MiB limit, exactly one each of `:20:` `:25:` `:28C:` `:60F:` `:62F:`, no multi-page (`:60M:`/`:62M:`) and no unknown tags, amounts held to NUMERIC(19,4), the statement must foot and every entry fall inside its period — every refusal is a 400 (`Mt940ParserTest`). The same account allow-list, Idempotency-Key, SHA-256 and (IBAN, statement id) uniqueness apply as for camt.053. **STRIDE-S residual (unchanged):** neither format is signature-verified; the uploader is recorded, the file is not authenticated. **STRIDE-D:** the upload records the new statement's breaks best-effort — a ledger that cannot answer that minute leaves the statement stored and the sweep records it later; one statement the ledger cannot answer for does not stop the sweep from ageing the rest. **Alerting:** `TreasuryNostroBreaksAged` (`max(openbank_treasury_nostro_breaks_aged) > 0 for 2h`, warning) — the gauge reads NaN on a cold pod until its first sweep, so a restart neither fires nor clears it (promtool test). **Residual:** a break resolves only by a later MATCH (the matcher still requires the same booking date); there is no manual "explain/close" action yet, and a statement older than 31 days is no longer re-reconciled, so its open breaks keep ageing until a person acts. Rollback: remove the two routes and the scheduler; V14 is additive (drop the table and its sequence).
- **2026-10-03** — **SENT outbox rows are purged after 7 days** (ADR-0329, ADR-0327 D8). `treasury_outbox` kept every SENT row, payload included, indefinitely: `purgeSent` existed and nothing called it. The shared libs-runtime `OutboxSentRetentionJob` now deletes SENT rows whose `sent_at` is older than `openbank.outbox.retention.sent-days` (default 7) nightly in bounded batches; the v1 repository opts in by delegating `SentOutboxRetention` to `PanacheOutboxRetention`. PENDING, FAILED, DISPATCHING and DEAD rows are never touched. Information disclosure: shrinks the window in which a database read exposes past deal-event payloads (counterparty data). No new endpoint, caller or privilege; replaying an event older than 7 days now comes from the broker or audit-service.
- **2026-10-03** — **Product limits enforced at submit and re-checked at approval (ADR-0315 D4, #11107).** D4 names three limit families checked before booking; until now only the counterparty limit existed. A product limit is declared as code in `openbank.treasury.product-limits` (per product: `max-principal` by currency — the currency set is the allowed list — and an optional `max-tenor-days`), evaluated by the pure `ProductLimitPolicy`. It is **fail-closed**: a product with no declared entry is not permitted at all, and an unknown product name in config refuses to boot, so a typo cannot leave a product unbounded. `Deal.submit` refuses a breach (the deal stays DRAFT) and `Deal.approve` re-evaluates the policy as declared at approval time — a mandate tightened between submit and approve refuses booking — with 422 `PRODUCT_LIMIT_BREACHED`, a human message and a structured `breaches` list (openapi 1.17.0 → 1.18.0, additive). No new endpoint, role or OPA action. **STRIDE-T (booking outside mandate):** closed in the domain, not in OPA, so it holds with `AUTHZ_ENFORCE` off; the check runs after the human, state and four-eyes checks (a self-approver still learns the four-eyes rule) and before the counterparty check. **STRIDE-E (override as bypass):** a product-limit breach is deliberately NOT overridable — the V4 senior override is bounded to counterparty exposure, which moves with other deals; a product mandate does not, and dealing outside it means changing the declared mandate under code review. A deal holding a counterparty override is still refused on a product breach (`TreasuryDealServiceTest`). **STRIDE-R:** `treasury.deal.booked.v1` gains an optional `productLimit` (decision `WITHIN_LIMIT`, the maximum principal in the deal's currency, the maximum tenor) — the mandate the booking was evaluated against (AsyncAPI 1.6.0, additive); a refusal changes no state, posts nothing and emits nothing (`TreasuryDealApiIT` 18/19). **Residual:** the check is not persisted at submit — the approver sees the refusal, not a recorded pre-check; there are no per-dealer authority limits (the realm has no dealer-level mandate to key them on); ADR-0313 risk limits remain unchecked. Rollback: revert the PR; no migration.
- **2026-10-10** — **Custodian statement of holdings and the period-end portfolio read (ADR-0337 amendment).** `POST /api/v1/treasury/portfolio/statements` ingests a custodian's ISO 20022 semt.002 (XML, required `Idempotency-Key`, OPA `treasury.portfolio.upload` — `ROLE_TREASURY_APPROVER`, human, never a service-account), parsed by openbank-libs-iso20022's `Semt002Reader` through the fleet's XXE-hardened `SecureXml` (DOCTYPE refused). It accepts only one complete page of a complete (`COMP`) statement for a configured safekeeping account of this deployment's entity. Every holding needs an ISO 6166-valid ISIN, a positive quantity, a non-negative valuation in one base currency, and a CFI that the configured prefix mapping classifies; otherwise the WHOLE statement is a 400 and nothing is stored. Figures the store would round are refused, not rounded. It posts nothing. **New inbound machine caller:** `GET /api/v1/treasury/portfolio/period-end?date=` (and the version trail `GET /portfolio/statements?date=`), OPA `treasury.portfolio.read`, is granted to treasury staff and to exactly ONE service-account, `service-account-openbank-tax-reporting` with ROLE_API, matched by `principal.id`. `treasury.portfolio.read` is listed in `rules.yaml: authz.operator_read_any_excluded_actions`, and base `rest.rego` now applies that list in compliance-read-any as well as operator-read-any, so the shared M2M account (ROLE_OPERATOR), any other service-account and any client holding ROLE_COMPLIANCE are denied. An AI agent is denied (no charter grants it). `treasury_rest_ext_test.rego` holds must-ALLOW and must-DENY cases, and three sabotages were run against them. **Tampering / repudiation:** stored versions are immutable. A correction for the same date is stored as the next version and only marks the prior one `superseded_by` / `superseded_at`, in the same transaction. A partial unique index admits one current version per (entity, date). Every version keeps the sha256 of the exact bytes and the uploader. **Disclosure:** the pension company's own investment holdings, not customer data. The read answers 409 when no statement exists, never an empty list, so a missing statement cannot be filed as an empty portfolio. **Residual:** the vendored semt.002.001.11 XSD is a working subset that only the fixtures are validated against (inbound files are read by local name). No `treasury-pension-co` deployment, NetworkPolicy ingress from tax-reporting, or realm client for it exists yet; those land with that instance.
