iOS TestFlight beta · volunteers

Join the mobile app beta

Test the OpenBank customer app on your iPhone through Apple TestFlight. The form below asks only for the Apple ID email your TestFlight uses, plus your consent to send the invite.

What we collect, and nothing else

Just two things: your Apple ID email, which Apple needs to send you a TestFlight invite, and your explicit consent via a checkbox you have to tick, which is the lawful basis (GDPR Art.6(1)(a)).

No name, no phone, no card, no device data, no analytics or tracking cookies. The email is stored only to send the invite, never shared, and deleted when the beta closes or you ask (just write to hello@open-bank.tech). The sandbox holds no real banking data. See the platform's regulatory coverage on the Platform page.

Submissions handled by Web3Forms (GDPR-compliant), with spam protection on. No tracking cookies.
