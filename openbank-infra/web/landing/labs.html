Inside openbank-libs

services share.

The domain core is plain Kotlin without framework dependencies. Shared types and rules help services agree at the source instead of reconciling differences later.

From OpenBank Labs · QRlessPay SDK

The QR payment.
Without the camera.

QRlessPay carries the same SPAYD payment descriptor a QR code would carry, directly from one phone to another over Bluetooth Low Energy. A bank-agnostic profile and public Swift/Kotlin SDK aim to let different banks and app platforms exchange a payment request with one tap.

The payer still sees the recipient and amount, confirms the proposal and authorizes the payment in their own bank app. Settlement stays on the bank's existing account-to-account rail.

Explore the SDK ↗Read the open wire spec ↗
